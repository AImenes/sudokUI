sudokUI

A free, open-source sudoku app for playing, practising and rating puzzles. It rates the difficulty of any sudoku with HoDoKu-compatible scores, knows 77 solving techniques with hints that explain every step, and has a practice mode for 65 of them, from X-Wings to Exocets. One shared daily puzzle, no ads, no account, works offline.
